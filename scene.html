Tab 02
7 Scene Types
Every 3-second frame falls into one of these 7 scene types. The Master Analysis Prompt automatically assigns the correct type based on what the script is saying at that moment.
🐸
TYPE 1 — Daddu Presenter
When: Direct address to viewer
Daddu stands on white background, pointing or gesturing. Used for hooks, pattern interrupts, closes, and CTA. Character occupies left or right 40% of frame.
💰
TYPE 2 — Single Icon Hero
When: One concept being named
One large flat icon centered on white background. Dollar bill, piggy bank, house, car, briefcase. Clean, minimal. Number or label below the icon in bold font.
⚖️
TYPE 3 — Comparison Split
When: Two options being compared
Two icons side by side with arrow or VS between them. Exact style of your competitor screenshot 1. Clean labels under each. No background color — pure white.
📊
TYPE 4 — Number/Stat Hero
When: Key number or stat revealed
Large bold number dominates center of white frame. Supporting label above or below in smaller font. Accent color on the number (green, red, gold). Nothing else.
➡️
TYPE 5 — Process Flow
When: Money or steps moving
Icons connected by arrows showing a process. Exact style of competitor screenshot 1 (money → savings account, money → HYSA). Horizontal or branching flow on white.
📈
TYPE 6 — Chart/Graph
When: Growth, comparison over time
Simple flat bar chart or line graph on white. Clean axes, 2–3 bars max. Color coding: green = good, red = bad, gray = average. Bold number labels on bars.
📋
TYPE 7 — List Item
When: Numbered list being revealed
Number (bold, large) + icon + label. One item per frame. Previous items shown smaller/faded in background to show progress. Clean, editorial, white background.

✕ Gradient backgrounds (white only)
✕ More than 3 icons per frame
✕ Decorative elements unrelated to the script
✕ Text longer than 6 words per frame

✕ Drop shadows on icons (flat style only)
✕ 3D elements or realistic rendering (except Daddu)
