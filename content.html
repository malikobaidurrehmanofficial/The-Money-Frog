Money frog details and information

What is The Money Frog?

The Money Frog (also known as the Three-Legged Toad or Chan Chu) is a popular Feng Shui symbol believed to attract wealth, good luck, and prosperity.

Key Details

  • 🪙 Usually depicted sitting on a pile of coins with a coin in its mouth.
  • 🦶 Has three legs — symbolising luck in wealth and abundance.
  • 🌑 Traditionally placed facing the door, but never pointing outward.
  • 🏠 Best placement: near the entrance, living room, or home office.

Tips for Use

Place the money frog in the wealth corner of your home (south-east in traditional Feng Shui). Keep it clean and treat it with respect. Remove the coin from its mouth at night and replace it in the morning to symbolise daily wealth attraction.
